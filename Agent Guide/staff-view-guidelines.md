Staff View

This is the dashboard for the staff members of Anaya. They can view all the bookings, appointments, and clients.

The navigation bar in this view is vertical and is located on the left side of the screen. It has the following pages:

Staff View Pages
1. Dashboard Home Page
2. Staff Appointment Management Page
3. Leave Request Page
4. Staff Profile Settings Page

Dashboard Home Page
    This page is the main dashboard for the staff members of Anaya. It displays all the important information about the staff members and their appointments. It also has a quick navigation to the other pages. Metrics displayed are:
    - Total Upcoming Appointments
    - Total Appointments Today
    - Total Service Duration Today

    ### Today's Appointments
    Directly below the metrics, there is a dedicated section displaying all appointments scheduled for the current day. This view provides a quick itinerary for the staff, showing the appointment times, customer identifiers, and the specific services to be rendered.

Staff Appointment Management Page
    It displays all the important information about the staff members and their appointments. It also has a quick navigation to the other pages.

    ### General Page Layout & Controls
    These controls apply globally across the appointment management interface, allowing staff to toggle between viewing active appointments and historical records.
    - **Views Available:**
      - **List View:** A chronological list format (default).
      - **Calendar View:** A monthly/weekly calendar highlighting days with appointments.
    - **Sorting & Filtering Options:**
      - **Sort by ID:** Ascending or Descending. (Default: Descending)
      - **Sort by Date:** Newest to oldest, or oldest to newest. (Default: Newest to Oldest)
      - **Filter by Status:** Upcoming, Finished, Cancelled, No Show. (Default: Upcoming)
      - **Filter by Service Type:** Narrow down the list to specific treatments (e.g., "Nail Care," "Facial Care"). (Default: All)
      - **Search Bar:** Quickly locate specific records by Customer Name or Appointment ID. (Default: Empty)

    ### Appointment Cards Data Reference
    The staff will see an overview of appointments containing the following fields (mirroring the customer view):
    - Appointment ID, Date, and Starting Time
    - Total Duration and Services included
    - Booking Status (Upcoming, Finished, Cancelled, No Show)
    - Downpayment Status (Pending, Paid)
    - Downpayment Total vs. Overall Balance
    - Time left to pay countdown (if pending)
    - **Quick Action Button:** An "Approve Appointment" button on the card.
    
    ### Appointment Detail Card (Payment Verification Flow)
    When a staff member presses the "Approve Appointment" (or "Confirm Appointment") button on a card, full appointment details open up in a modal or dedicated view.
    - **Payment Details Section:** Within this view, staff can review the customer's payment info (Reference Number, Account Name) and view the attached **GCash receipt screenshot**.
    - **Final Action:** Scrolling down to the bottom of the appointment details card, the staff member must click either **"Confirm Appointment"** or **"Deny Appointment"** based on the validity of the GCash receipt.

    ### Appointment History
    Staff members can switch the primary view to access a comprehensive history of their past appointments. This view utilizes the **General Page Layout & Controls** defined above (filtering by "Finished," "Cancelled," or "No Show" statuses) to audit past performance and schedule loads.

Leave Request Page
    This page is where staff members can make a leave request. It also has a quick navigation to the other pages.

    Leave Request Area has the following Inputs:
    - Leave Type (Buttons)
        - Vacation Leave
        - Sick Leave
        - Emergency Leave
        - Others
    - Start Date
    - End Date
    - Reason
    - Submit Button

Staff Profile Settings Page
    This page is where staff members can view and edit their profile information. It also has a quick navigation to the other pages. Staffs can change and edit the following information:
    
    - Profile Picture
    - Name
    - Password
    
    Contact & Social
        - Contact Number
        - Email Address
        - Social Media Links
